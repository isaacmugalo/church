The Sheep and Shepherd Association

Established in 2026 by the Gospel Disciples Church, the Sheep and Shepherd Association is built on the foundation of community and mutual support. The association aims to empower both church members and non-members financially.

By joining the association, you and your loved ones gain access to a reliable financial safety net during life’s major milestones and challenging times.


🎁 Association Benefits Summary

🎉 Life Celebrations

Members receive a contribution of Ksh 1,000 from every registered member to help support events like weddings and graduations.

🕊️ Bereavement Support

In the event of a loss, members and their officially listed next of kin are eligible to receive financial support from the group.
